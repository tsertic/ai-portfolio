The Human Behind the Machine

Based in the historical heart of Zagreb, Croatia, I navigate the labyrinthine complexities of full-stack development with a particular affinity for enterprise-grade architecture. By day, I am a central cog at Arctis d.o.o., where I orchestrate technical solutions for formidable global entities such as Saudi Aramco and the European Space Agency. My expertise lies in the Archibus IWMS ecosystem—a platform where I bridge the chasm between legacy Java backends and contemporary React frontends, ensuring stability across SQL databases and Tomcat servers.

When the enterprise sun sets, I transition into a digital artisan, crafting bespoke Next.js and TypeScript environments for a diverse clientele. Whether it's optimizing a mission-critical database query or breathing 3D life into a bakery website with Three.js, my philosophy remains constant: every technical problem is a puzzle awaiting an intellectual solution. Outside the terminal, life is punctuated by the energy of my daughter Anja and the curious spirit of Eevee, my mixed-breed canine companion.
